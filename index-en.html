Core Features

Data Security

Native OpenZFS support, providing enterprise-grade data protection with snapshots, checksums, auto-healing, compression, and deduplication.

Simple & Reliable

OneNAS is developed based on Debian 13, with simple permission control, no professional knowledge required, fully controllable industrial-grade storage.

Rich Plugins

One-click installation of Docker, Plex, Nextcloud, and 100+ applications, easily extending NAS functionality.

Simple & Focused
